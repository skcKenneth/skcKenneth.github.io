STUDENT CLASSROOM GUIDE
Mixing tank: can a smooth curve be trusted?
What does agreement with an analytic solution check, and what does it leave
untested?
50 minutes · Introductory modeling · pairs
Before you begin
Derivatives, units, Python functions and arrays. This is a 50-minute introduction to the existing
approximately 150-minute workshop, not its completion.
The well-mixed tank is an invented teaching system. No measured equipment, mixing
validation or plant-safety result is supplied.
One-class sequence
5 min 01. Predict the long-run level
Sketch a 20 L tank: 2 L/min enters at 10 g/L; equal outflow, reaction 0.05/min, initial
concentration 0. Will concentration reach 10 g/L?
10 min 02. State assumptions and balance mass
Write accumulation as inflow minus outflow minus reaction. Explain why volume is constant
and outlet concentration equals tank concentration; check units.

10 min 03. Check two steps, then implement
Use the initial-value problem below to calculate two Euler steps. Complete student_model

15 min 04. Run, change one condition, refine
Run the default, then set reaction to 0.2 per minute. Return to default physical parameters
and halve the time step to 0.25 minutes. Record your actual run’s errors and checks,
including failures. If time permits, try zero reaction.
5 min 05. Separate two kinds of evidence
What would an analytic-solution match tell you? Propose one tracer observation that would
challenge the perfect-mixing assumption.
5 min 06. Leave a short evidence record
Write one result, one check and one limitation. Answer the exit question and export the
original workshop’s Draft JSON or report; do not mark the full workshop complete.
