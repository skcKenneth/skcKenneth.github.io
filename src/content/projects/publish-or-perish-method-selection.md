## Editorial overview

Read [When Publication Count Selects the Method](/writing/when-publication-count-selects-the-method/) for a full account of the agent rules, policy grid, reliability-throughput trade-off, publication-bias sweep, and five approved figures.

This is not a duplicate of the earlier field-reliability model. That study varies novelty, rigour, and replication incentives; this one isolates cultural selection on methodological effort under biased publication and consequential audits. All executable materials remain private.
