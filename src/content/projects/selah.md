[Start the interactive lesson](/teaching/selah/)

Selah opens with a Senior Division problem from the 2023/2024 Macau Interschool Mathematics Competition. Listen to the proof in English or Cantonese as the diagram unfolds. Move the point, pause on the sloping line, and try a new interval on your own.

During the invited pilot, select a formula or point and ask the tutor about what you see. Resume at the same moment after a follow-up. The library also includes lessons on logarithms and quadrilateral diagonals. Paste a problem or check the text from a photo or one-page PDF to look for a matching lesson.

The name comes from [Psalm 3:4 in the King James Version](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+3%3A4&version=KJV). For this project, it suggests taking a moment to think before moving on.
