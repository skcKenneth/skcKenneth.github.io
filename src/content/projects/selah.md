[Start the interactive lesson](/teaching/selah/)

Selah opens with a Senior Division problem from the 2023/2024 Macau Interschool Mathematics Competition. Move a point along a quarter circle, predict when the sum of two square roots is largest, and use two short checks to build the proof.

The lesson and its hints are open to everyone. During the invited pilot, students can ask an AI tutor a text question about the current step and continue from the same board.

The name comes from [Psalm 3:4 in the King James Version](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+3%3A4&version=KJV). For this project, it suggests taking a moment to think before moving on.
